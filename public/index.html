      Agents

      An agent is a helper with its own job, way of talking and rules, and a clear limit on which memories it can see. An AI becomes that agent when you connect it to the agent, or when you paste the agent’s instructions into it.

      To give an AI app its own agent, choose Copy connection settings and paste them into that app. The agent is fixed by those settings, never by anything the AI says, so an AI cannot give itself more access.

      Secure Vault

      A locked box for passwords, keys, ID numbers and payment details. Each item is locked on its own, using a key made from your master password. The password itself is never saved.

        Backups
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
          
        Lock your backups with a passphrase
        
          
        A passphrase locks a backup before it leaves this computer (for example, to Google Drive). If you lose it, those backups cannot be opened, and nobody can recover it for you. It is kept in a private file on this computer, outside your vault.

        Your devices
        
        
        Use one memory on all your computers. Each one keeps its own copy, and they keep each other up to date through a folder you share between them (Syncthing, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive or your own server). Everything in that folder is locked with your sync passphrase. How to set it up (opens in a new tab)
        
        
        You are on a phone or tablet. To change these settings, change a passphrase or add a phone, use the dashboard on your computer.
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
          
        
          
        
          
        
            
        
        
        
        
        
          
        Sync passphrase
        
          
        Use the same passphrase on every computer. Anyone who has the shared folder and this passphrase can read your memories, so keep it private. It cannot be recovered. It is kept in a private file on this computer, outside your vault.

Each one signs in with a one-time code and gets a key of its own.

          Delete everything?
          
      
          This removes every note, conversation and work log from your vault, and the readable copies of them. A backup is made first, so you can still get it back with the command line (memvault backup keeps your backups). Agents and Secure Vault items are not deleted.
          
      
      
      
          
      
          
    
          
            

  
    
          
      
          Link a machine
          
      
          Control another MemVault (a VPS, a work computer) from this dashboard, and from any phone signed in here. On that machine, turn on the server workspace and make a pairing code: memvault devices pair --name "This laptop", or Settings → Add a phone.
