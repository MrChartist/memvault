Welcome. Here is how MemVault works

MemVault is a notebook on your own computer that all your AI assistants can share. Tell one AI something once, and the others can find it too.

  1. Connect your AI app Works with Claude, Cursor, Antigravity, VS Code and other AI apps that can connect to tools.

Agents

An agent is a helper with its own job, way of talking and rules, and a clear limit on which memories it can see. An AI becomes that helper when you connect it to the helper, or when you paste the helper's instructions into it.

To give an AI app its own helper, choose Copy connection settings and paste them into that app. The helper is fixed by those settings, never by anything the AI says, so an AI cannot give itself more access.

Secure Vault

A locked box for passwords, keys, ID numbers and payment details. Each item is locked on its own, using a key made from your master password. The password itself is never saved.

Backups

Lock your backups with a passphrase

A passphrase locks a backup before it leaves this computer (for example, to Google Drive). If you lose it, those backups cannot be opened, and nobody can recover it for you. It is kept in a private file on this computer, outside your vault.

Your projects

Tell MemVault about the things you work on. Notes that mention these words are labelled with the project automatically. This applies to new notes after your AI apps are restarted.

Your data
