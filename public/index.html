Welcome. Here is how MemVault works

MemVault is a notebook on your own computer that all your AI assistants can share. Tell one AI something once, and the others can find it too.

  1. Connect your AI app Works with Claude, Cursor, Antigravity, VS Code and any app that supports MCP.
  2. Choose helpers (optional) An agent is a helper with its own job, voice and rules, such as a study helper or a writing helper.

Your privacy, in plain words. Your notes are stored only on this computer, and MemVault sends nothing anywhere by itself. When you ask an AI to look something up here, the part it reads goes to that AI's company, like anything else you type to it. Passwords and ID numbers are masked before they are saved.

Agents

An agent is a helper with its own job, voice and rules, and a clear limit on which memories it can see. Any AI becomes that helper when it calls agent_activate, or when you paste the briefing into it.

To give an AI app its own agent, choose Copy MCP config and paste it into that app. The agent is set by that setting, never by anything the AI says, so an AI cannot give itself more access.

Secure Vault

A locked box for passwords, API keys, IDs and payment details. Each item is locked on its own with strong encryption (AES-256), using a key made from your master password. The password itself is never saved.

First time? The password you type now becomes your master password. Use at least 10 characters. If you forget it, there is no way to recover what is inside.
