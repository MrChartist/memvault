The problem

Every AI chat starts with an empty head

You explain who you are, what you are working on and how you like answers. Then you open another app and do it all again. MemVault is one notebook that all your AI apps share, so you say it once.

You, in Claude
“Remember that I prefer short answers with one example.”
Later, you, in Cursor
“Help me write this email.”
Cursor
Looked in MemVault
“Here is a short draft with one example…”

Three steps

  1. Set it up on your computer

    One setup command creates your private vault in your home folder. Nothing goes to a server.

  2. Connect your AI apps

    MemVault prints the settings to paste into each app. The dashboard's Get started card walks you through it.

  3. Talk as you normally do

    Say “remember…” in one app. Any connected app can find it later, and you can see and correct every note in the dashboard.

Agents

A helper for each job, with its own view

An agent is a saved profile: who the helper is, how it should sound, what it must always and never do, and which memories it may see. A study helper does not need your work notes.

  • Everyday AssistantPlain-language help with questions and small jobs
  • Study BuddyExplains step by step and lets the learner try first
  • Writing HelperPlans and edits while keeping your own voice
  • PlannerTurns goals into small, realistic steps
  • ResearcherSeparates what is known from what is assumed
  • Software EngineerSmall, tested changes that match your code
  • CoordinatorSplits a goal and hands each part to the right helper

Or describe a new helper in your own words in the dashboard.

Memory spaces and who can read them
Memory spaceWho can read it
sharedEvery agent: the common memory
agent:<name>Only that agent, and you
project:<name>Agents you allow, for one project

An AI cannot give itself more access. Each app is tied to one agent by its own settings, never by anything the AI says. Notes an agent may not see are not just hidden; they are not in its copy of the memory at all.

All your devices

One memory on your laptop, your PC, your server and your phone

Each computer keeps its own full copy, so your AI apps work offline. They keep each other up to date through a folder you share between them. Every file in that folder is encrypted with your passphrase.

  • With or without a server. A VPS that is always on keeps everyone in step. With no server, your computers update each other directly whenever two of them are on.
  • Use the folder tool you like. Syncthing (free, device to device), iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive. They only ever see encrypted files.
  • The newest change wins everywhere. Deletions travel too, and a sync that would delete many notes makes a backup first.
  • Your iPhone, privately. Open the dashboard through Tailscale, with a one-time code. A lost phone can be signed out on its own.

Privacy and safety

Your notes stay with you, and we say plainly what that means

You can check all of this on your own computer: run memvault doctor, or open the Security page in the dashboard.

Not reachable from outside

The server listens on 127.0.0.1 only. Every request needs a secret key, and web pages from other sites are refused.

Secrets hidden before saving

Common API keys, passwords, card numbers, one-time codes and national ID numbers are replaced with a marker. It is pattern matching, so unusual formats can be missed.

Each AI sees only its share

Agents read through a filtered copy of the memory, and are offered only the tools they may use.

Cloud copies encrypted first

Without your passphrase, a cloud backup refuses to run. It never uploads plain text by default.

See who used your memory

A tamper-evident log records which agent did what, and when. It never records the content.

Off until you switch it on

Capture of git, files, browser pages, the clipboard and more is off until you switch it on. No analytics, no telemetry.

What no tool can promise

When you ask an AI app to look something up in MemVault, the text it reads is sent to the company behind that AI, just like anything you type to it. Agents limit how much each AI can see; they cannot change that.

  • Masking catches common patterns. It is a safety net, not a guarantee.
  • Malware running as your own user account can read your files. Turn on disk encryption (BitLocker, FileVault or LUKS) to protect a lost laptop.
  • A forgotten master password or backup passphrase cannot be recovered. There is no reset, by design.

The full list is in SECURITY.md.

Get started

Set it up in a few minutes

You need Node.js 20 or newer (choose the “LTS” download) and a terminal. A one-click installer is planned but not built yet.

Works today

Install from the source code

Paste these lines into a terminal, one after another.

Terminal
git clone https://github.com/MrChartist/memvault.git
cd memvault
npm install
node cli.mjs setup       # one step, no questions
node cli.mjs mcp-config  # settings for your AI app
node cli.mjs open        # opens the dashboard
  • Node.js 20 or newer
  • No account
  • No cloud service

Coming soon
After the first release on npm, setup becomes one line: npx @mrchartist/memvault setup

Connect an AI app

Paste what mcp-config prints into the app's MCP settings, then restart the app.

Before you start

Known limits, so you do not find them the hard way

  • Needs a terminal todaySetup uses Node.js and a few commands. A one-click installer is on the roadmap.
  • Built and used on LinuxAutomated tests pass on Windows and macOS too, but nobody has used it on those systems by hand yet.
  • Search finds words, not meaning“car” will not find “automobile”. Chinese, Japanese and Korean match parts of words.
  • A few tens of thousands of notesAbout 10,000 notes feel instant. At 100,000, each save takes up to about two seconds.
  • English interfaceYour notes can be in any language and any direction. The dashboard text is English only for now.
  • Checked by tools, not yet by peopleAccessibility is tested automatically on every screen. A review with real screen-reader users is planned.

Questions

Common questions

Is MemVault free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT licence. There is no account, no subscription and no paid tier.

Do my notes leave my computer?

They are stored only on your computer. There are two exceptions you control: when an AI app reads a note, that text goes to the company behind that AI; and if you switch on Google Drive backups, an encrypted copy goes to your Drive.

Do I need to be a programmer?

No, but today you need to install Node.js and paste a few commands into a terminal. After that, everything is in the dashboard: notes, agents, backups and settings.

Can I use it on more than one computer, and on my phone?

Yes. Each computer keeps its own copy and they stay in step through a folder you share between them (Syncthing, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive or your own server). Everything in that folder is encrypted with your sync passphrase.

Your iPhone or iPad opens the dashboard of a computer or server that is switched on, through a private Tailscale address. Claude on the iPhone cannot read MemVault yet. See the devices guide.

Which AI apps can use it?

Any app that supports MCP servers, including Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Antigravity, and VS Code with Cline or Roo. Each app can be tied to its own agent.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Items in the Secure Vault cannot be opened again, and the same is true for an encrypted backup if you lose its passphrase. This is on purpose: there is no back door. Write them down somewhere safe.

Can I bring my old ChatGPT or Claude chats?

Yes. Download your export from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini (Google Takeout) or Perplexity, then run memvault import on the folder. Each conversation keeps its own date. Services change their export formats without notice, so please report a file that does not import.

How do I remove it?

Removing the program does not remove your notes. The uninstall guide lists everything MemVault leaves, so you can decide what to keep.
